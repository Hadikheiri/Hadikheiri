### Hi, I'm Hadi

I'm a Geoinformatics Engineering master's student at Politecnico di Milano, and since October 2026 I've been in Bonn writing my thesis at the University of Bonn. Most of what I work on comes down to one question: can we trust this data?

**Right now:** soil-moisture sensor networks. I link each station to its neighbours on a Delaunay graph, compare them with Dynamic Time Warping and look for the ones that don't fit. The thesis code isn't public yet.
